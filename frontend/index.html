Report → Group → Link → Prioritise → Act → Verify

One accountable path from a citizen's report to a resolved problem

Each step is recorded with who did it and when. Officers stay in control: AI only suggests.

  1. 1
    Report
    Citizen pins the location, describes it, optionally adds a photo
  2. 2
    Group
    Nearby related reports become one incident
  3. 3
    Link
    Matched to a compatible asset within 150 m
  4. 4
    Prioritise
    Bounded 0–100 score with visible factors
  5. 5
    Act
    Officer approves, edits or rejects the plan
  6. 6
    Verify
    Proof photo + recurrence check before closing
🧩

Incident grouping

Reports of the same or closely related category within 1.5 km and 48 h are grouped. A pothole is never merged with a streetlight just because it's nearby.

🏗️

Infrastructure intelligence

Complaints are linked to the specific streetlight, road segment or drain they concern, building a maintenance and complaint history per asset.

🚩

Risk findings

Rules flag repeated complaints on one asset, recurrence after a repair, critical assets, overdue inspections and conflicting work orders — each with its evidence.

📊

Explainable priority

Severity, report count, time unresolved, category safety weight and asset criticality add up to a score officers can read and challenge.

✅

Accountable resolution

Work can only close with an approved plan and a proof photo that passes automated checks. The officer's name is recorded and citizens are notified.

🔒

Privacy by design

Citizens see only their own reports. Photos are private. Public tracking needs a private code and reveals only status.

What this prototype does — and doesn't — claim

  • Classification and plan drafting use Sarvam AI when configured, otherwise a transparent rule-based engine. Every result shows which one ran.
  • Photo checks detect invalid, blank, tiny or re-used images. They are not an independent field inspection.
  • Ward assignment uses the nearest configured ward centre, not official ward boundaries.
  • It is not connected to any municipal system. Demo data is fictional and labelled as synthetic.
